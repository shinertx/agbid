# AgBid

**The Uber for crop chemicals.** A grower-facing farm input ordering platform that lets farmers request products at specific volumes and receive competitive blind bids from distributors.

## What is AgBid?

AgBid is a reverse-auction marketplace for agricultural crop protection inputs (herbicides, fungicides, insecticides). Farmers broadcast what they need, how much, and when — and signed-up distributors compete on price, delivery speed, and adjuvant bundles.

### How it works (3 taps):
1. **Search** — Farmer picks a product (e.g., "Roundup") 
2. **Specify** — How many gallons, and delivery deadline
3. **Get Quotes** — Distributors submit blind bids within 48 hours
4. **Pick** — Farmer compares on price, delivery, or total cost and selects the winner

### Key mechanics:
- **Active Ingredient Parity Engine** — Maps brand names (Roundup, Touchdown, Durango) to their raw active ingredient (Glyphosate) so distributors compete on substance, not labels
- **Must-Pick-A-Bid Commitment** — Farmers must select a bid to prevent free price-discovery leakage
- **Clearinghouse (Option A)** — AgBid acts as Merchant of Record with integrated ag-lender financing (John Deere Operating Lines, Rabobank)
- **Adjuvant Bundling** — Distributors can offer a price with or without their proprietary adjuvant
- **2.5% Take Rate** — Platform fee on cleared transactions

## Design Philosophy

- **Farmer-first, not engineer-first.** No jargon. No dashboards. No "RFQs."
- **Mobile-first.** Farmers are in trucks and on phones.
- **Three taps to a quote.** Search → Specify → Get Quotes.
- **No ads on Day 1.** Trust before monetization.

## Label Verification

```bash
python3 scripts/verify_epa_labels.py
```

The app prefers `agbid_label_database.verified.json` when it exists. Products marked `needs_manual_review` are acceptable for discovery and demo flows, but require manual label/compliance review before any live Texas transaction.

## EPA PPIS Launch-Wedge Index

```bash
python3 scripts/build_ppis_index.py
```

This builds `outputs/ppis_launch_wedge_index.json` and `outputs/ppis_launch_wedge_summary.json` from official EPA PPIS files. The operator board and `/api/labels/ppis-summary` use the summary to show how much official product coverage exists for the launch active-ingredient wedge. PPIS proves product identity and active-ingredient parity; PPLS label review and Texas compliance gates are still required before a live transaction clears.

## Startup Foundry Gate

AgBid is managed as a clearing wedge, not a quote demo. The live operator board includes a Startup Foundry Gate that scores the current state against:

- Structural moat from verified labels, active-ingredient parity, and transaction data.
- Bypass prevention from must-pick behavior and AgBid clearing.
- Incumbent vulnerability from distributor channel conflict.
- Distribution proof from one committed grower ask and three freight-included supplier commitments.
- Cash-flow proof from a post-award deal review that is ready for manual clearing.

The gate can say `demo_mode`, `field_test_ready`, `transaction_proof_running`, or `manual_clearing_ready`. Treat `manual_clearing_ready` as a software/operator readiness signal only; legal, payment, compliance, insurance, and first live transaction proof still decide whether AgBid is actually launched.

## First Principles Score: 9/10

The software can reach 9/10 in the Startup Foundry gate after a clearing-ready proof run. The company is not fully launched until a real Texas grower and real suppliers complete a live, freight-included, must-pick transaction through manual clearing.
