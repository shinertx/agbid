# Texas Pilot Recruiting Packet

## Objective

Prove or kill AgBid with one live Texas chemical-buying cycle:

1. One grower submits a real request for 250+ gallons.
2. Three Texas-capable suppliers submit blind delivered bids.
3. At least one delivered bid beats the grower's hidden target price.
4. The grower selects a bid through AgBid.
5. AgBid manually reviews label, payment, delivery, and compliance before money moves.

## Grower ICP

Start with Texas row-crop operators who already buy bulk crop inputs and care more about delivered price than coop hand-holding.

Good first growers:

- 2,000+ acres
- Buys 250+ gallons of common herbicides
- Uses spreadsheets, texts, or calls multiple dealers today
- Has an operating line or cash/wire capacity
- Will give product, gallons, delivery ZIP, need-by date, and lock-in target price

Bad first growers:

- Small hobby acreage
- Needs agronomic advice more than price competition
- Only wants a free benchmark quote
- Cannot commit to selecting a winning bid

## Supplier ICP

Start with suppliers who can win by moving volume without publicly advertising a price board.

Good first suppliers:

- Can deliver 250+ gallon totes or bulk equivalents in Texas
- Will quote freight-included delivered totals
- Can provide EPA registration number on every product
- Will optionally bundle adjuvant economics
- Understands AgBid is the clearing party in the pilot

Capture supplier readiness through `supplier_onboarding.html` before inviting the supplier into real pilot requests.

Bad first suppliers:

- Refuses blind bidding
- Wants to see the grower's hidden target
- Will not include freight
- Will not provide EPA registration number
- Wants to bypass AgBid immediately

## Discovery Questions For Growers

Ask about past behavior, not interest:

- What crop chemical did you buy most recently?
- How many gallons did you buy?
- What did you pay per gallon and delivered total?
- How many dealers did you call before buying?
- Did freight change the decision?
- What product would you buy again this month or this season?
- What delivered price would make you lock it in?
- If a supplier beats that price, are you willing to pick the bid through AgBid?

## Discovery Questions For Suppliers

- What products can you deliver in 250+ gallon quantities in Texas?
- Which Texas regions can you serve this month?
- Can you include freight in one delivered total?
- Can you submit a blind bid without seeing other bids?
- If AgBid is buyer/clearing party, are you willing to bid?
- Can you provide EPA registration number and current label link for every bid?
- Would you include an optional adjuvant bundle and show both totals?

## Pilot Pass Standard

Proceed if:

- One grower submits a real request with hidden reserve.
- Three suppliers submit delivered bids.
- One supplier bid beats the reserve.
- Grower selects a bid.
- Supplier accepts AgBid as clearing party.

## Pilot Kill Standard

Kill or pivot if:

- Suppliers refuse blind delivered bids even with AgBid as clearing party.
- Growers refuse must-pick commitment after setting a target price.
- Freight makes cross-supplier savings disappear.
- Label/Texas compliance review blocks the most common products in the launch wedge.

## Operator Note

Do not sell this as an app yet. Sell it as a managed buying desk: “Tell us what you need, how many gallons, when you need it, and your lock-in price. We will bring back blind delivered bids from Texas-capable suppliers.”

Use `operator.html` as the live pilot board for supplier readiness, label confidence, request status, and Texas compliance gates.

Use `pilot_leads.html` as the live recruiting desk. Every grower or supplier conversation should create or update a lead with contact, region, product interest, next action, and the specific pilot gate it satisfies. Supplier onboarding and submitted grower requests automatically create pilot leads, but operator-entered outreach leads still belong here too. Do not count a grower as real unless they provide product, gallons, delivery ZIP, and lock-in price. Do not count a supplier as real unless they accept freight-included blind bids and AgBid clearing.
